# Capstone 1 

Sales Performance analysis project using SQL and the sample_sales database.

## Sales Territory
Sales Manager: Jim Heck
State: Colorado
Region: West

## Project Overview
  The purpose of this project was to analyze sales and information regarding Jim Heck's Colorado EmporiUm stores using the sample_sales database.
Using SQL, I examined sales revenue, trends over the months and years, average sales, and I also ranked how well his 18 stores were doing compared to each other. I used my findings to identify important business trends and insights.
  I exported my SQL findings into Microsoft Excel to create a clean visual representing my data insights. I organized the insights using worksheets with PivotTables and charts.
    The goal of this project is to provide Jim Heck with a clearer understanding of the sales and performance of his stores by identifying trends and highlighting key information for improving EmporiUm throughout Colorado.

## Analysis Questions & Key Findings
### Question 1: Total Colorado Territory Revenue
Total Colorado revenue from 2022-2025 was **$6,412,632.22** across 18 EmporiUm stores.
### Question 2: Monthly Revenue Trends (2022-2025)
Revenue increased each year, growing from **$1,011,355.52 in 2022** to **$2,421,918.17 in 2025**. This indicates strong growth in Colorado sales throughout the four-year period.
### Question 3: Colorado Revenue Compared to Online Sales
Colorado generated **$6,412,632.22** in revenue, while total online sales reached **$68,004,827.05**. Online sales had a 10.6x in revenue over Colorado.
### Question 4: Category Transaction Analysis
Across 2022-2025, Technology and Accessories had the highest average transaction value, while books (General) had the lowest.
### Question 5: Colorado Store Revenue Rankings
Store 705 ranked first in Colorado with **$917,471.53** in revenue, followed by Store 709 with **$540,358.11**. Store 705 was the highest performing location.
### Question 6: Store Performance Compared to the Average
The average Colorado EmporiUm revenue was **$356,257,35**. Two stores performed above average, while sixteen stores were below average.
